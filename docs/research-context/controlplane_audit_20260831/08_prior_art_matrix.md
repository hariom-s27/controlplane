## §8A Adversarial prior-art challenge

Each proposed ControlPlane distinction was actively attacked with alternate terminology (*pre-action authorization, action gating, admission control, policy decision point, runtime enforcement, trace grounding, provenance binding, evidence binding, pre-execution guardrail*) and by searching for the mechanism rather than the name.

| Proposed distinction | Counterexample found | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-execution interception of tool calls | Reddy et al.; OAP; AgentCore Policy; Policies on Paths (prospective); AgentLTL (online); C-Trace | **SUBSTANTIALLY OVERLAPPING** |
| Deterministic, non-LLM adjudication | Reddy et al.; AgentCore/Cedar; Automated Reasoning checks (SMT) | **SUBSTANTIALLY OVERLAPPING** |
| Declarative / data-driven policy | Cedar & Dogwood; FO-LTL; C-Trace's first-order predicates | **SUBSTANTIALLY OVERLAPPING** — and ControlPlane's JDM expression list is the *least* expressive of these |
| Signed audit receipt per decision | OAP ("cryptographically signed audit record"); Aegis (Immutable Logging Kernel) | **SUBSTANTIALLY OVERLAPPING**, and both cited systems are cryptographically stronger |
| Consulting external state at decision time | Policies on Paths (governance ledger Σ); AgentCore Guardrails (inline information providers); Reddy et al. ("and current state") | **PARTIALLY OVERLAPPING** |
| **Adjudicating the claim against the versioned business record the claim is about, including silent supersession** | None found. AgentLTL is trace-internal; Automated Reasoning checks explicitly consults no database; AgentCore's documented inputs are identity/schema/session/content-safety; Reddy et al.'s state is a simulated benchmark DB with no version history | **CLEARLY DISTINGUISHED** — with the honesty caveat that this is a *design position*, evidenced by one scenario |
| **Per-field (not per-system) source-reliability classes feeding a verdict floor** | None found in the searched corpus | **PARTIALLY OVERLAPPING / NOT ESTABLISHED** — no counterexample found, but the mechanism is **not wired** in ControlPlane either (`escalation_for()` is dead code), so nothing is demonstrated |
| Checkability tiering of claims (C1–C5) | Not found under that name; conceptually adjacent to verification-moment and formal-grounding axes in the safe-agents survey (2608.14590 §4) | **PARTIALLY OVERLAPPING** — plausibly a useful taxonomy contribution, but taxonomies are weak contributions absent evidence they change decisions |

**Two counterexamples that would weaken the surviving claim if read in full, and were not:** the OAP specification repository (`aport.io/spec/`) may define record-validation predicates the abstract does not mention; and C-Trace's Table 8 was not rendered. Both are **open counterarguments and are recorded as such** rather than resolved in ControlPlane's favour.

## §12A notice

**No numeric comparison may be drawn between ControlPlane and any system in this matrix.** Reddy et al. report Δ task-success on τ²-bench airline; OAP reports attack success over authorization attempts; C-Trace reports attack success and FPR under extractor noise; ControlPlane reports verdict accuracy on a self-generated corpus. Metric definitions, numerators, denominators, units of analysis, populations and protocols all differ. Classification: **NOT DIRECTLY COMPARABLE.**
