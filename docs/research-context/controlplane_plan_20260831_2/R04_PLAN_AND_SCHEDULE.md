## The decision your flowchart asks for

> *"Is a working tool-call experiment still worth doing? NO → close the Kimi track. YES → new track."*

**Answer: YES — but the gate is the probe result, not a strategic guess, and it is no longer the load-bearing experiment.**

- If the probe says the fault is config or adapter: run the new track. It is cheap and it buys external validity.
- If the probe says model/provider: **close the Kimi track with a documented limitation, and do not switch models before the deadline.** The A-ladder (Phase 2) answers your actual research question without a provider, and closing on an established infrastructure limitation is a clean, honest outcome.

Either way, **Phase 2 is not conditional on any of it**, which is the point of the reframe in `R03` §5.
